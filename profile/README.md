## What we build

- **Weather and agro-advice for farmers.** Forecasts from several sources with history, frost and spraying advice, planting windows, delivered through a Telegram bot.
- **Market information.** Prices of agricultural products on the markets of Central Asia, collected every week.
- **Agricultural maps and libraries.** Open data and knowledge for farmers, advisors and partners.
- **Websites and online stores** for the group and for our clients.

## How we work

- Every site works in **Tajik, Russian and English**.
- Pages are fast and accessible: **Lighthouse 100** in every category is the bar for each page.
- We respect visitors' privacy: statistics load only after the visitor acts on the page, and never when the browser asks not to be tracked.
- Changes go through pull requests, automated checks and reviews before they reach production.
